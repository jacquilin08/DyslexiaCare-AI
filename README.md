# 🧠 DyslexiaCare AI

### AI-Powered Personalized Learning & Reading Support for Learners with Dyslexia

DyslexiaCare AI is an AI-powered learning support platform designed to help learners with dyslexia improve their **reading, comprehension, pronunciation, spelling, and writing skills** through personalized and adaptive learning experiences.

Instead of providing the same learning experience to every student, DyslexiaCare AI adapts activities and learning support based on the learner's performance and needs.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Learners with dyslexia often face difficulties with reading fluency, word recognition, spelling, comprehension, pronunciation, and letter/word confusion.

Traditional learning platforms generally provide the same content and difficulty level to all learners, making it difficult to provide individualized support.

**DyslexiaCare AI addresses this gap by combining AI-assisted learning, personalized activities, accessibility features, and gamification in a single platform.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

DyslexiaCare AI provides an interactive learning environment where learners can:

* Assess their reading and learning difficulties
* Follow a personalized learning path
* Practice commonly confused letters and words
* Improve pronunciation through an AI reading coach
* Improve spelling and writing skills
* Simplify complex learning content
* Test their understanding through interactive activities
* Track their learning progress
* Learn through gamified levels and challenges

---

## ⭐ Core Innovation

### AI Comprehension Companion

The core innovation of DyslexiaCare AI is an **AI-powered comprehension companion** that goes beyond simply reading text aloud.

It helps learners understand difficult content by providing:

**Dynamic Text Simplification**
Learners can adjust the complexity of text according to their reading ability while preserving the original meaning.

**Context-Based Explanation**
Difficult words, sentences, and concepts can be explained in simpler language.

**Comprehension Checks**
The system can ask short questions after reading to determine whether the learner actually understood the content.

**Personalized Support**
The learning experience can adapt based on the learner's performance instead of following a fixed difficulty level.
